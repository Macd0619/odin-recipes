Banana Loaf Cake

Home

Description

This banana cake recipe is easy and a family favorite. It is great for using overripe bananas to create a super-moist cake. You can also add nuts of your choice, if you wish.

Preparation time:15 minutes Cook time: 50 minutes Total time: 1 hour 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas
  • 1 1/2 cups All-purpose flour
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1/4 cup soft butter
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Grease and flour a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Mash ripe bananas in a large bowl. Add the rest of the ingredients to the mashed bananas; mix until well combined. Pour into the prepared loaf pan.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until a toothpick inserted into the center of loaf comes out clean, 50 to 60 minutes.
