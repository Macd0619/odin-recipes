Tandoori Chicken

Home

Description

Try this delicious, mouth-watering chicken with a blend of spices that you and the family will savour.

Cooking time: 25 minutes

Servings: 2 to 3. You can add more ingredients to suit additional servings.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 5ml coriander(dhania) powder
  • 10ml tandoori masala
  • 5ml cumin(jeera) powder
  • 5ml salt
  • 10ml crushed ginger and garlic(fresh))
  • 60ml tomato paste
  • 80ml plain yogurt
  • 10ml lemon juice
  • 50gr butter

Steps

  1. Wash and cut chicken in two pieces or as desired
  2. In a separate bowl, add and mix rest of the ingredients, except for the butter
  3. Coat/smear the chicken pieces with the spice mixture and allow to marinade for approximately 5 to 6 hours. N.B. Leave aside some of the spice mixture to baste chicken further during grilling
  4. Switch on oven to grill
  5. Place chicken pieces in grilling tray
  6. Coat the chicken pieces again with some of the spice mixture and dot with pieces of butter. Grill for approximately 15 minutes
  7. During grilling, baste chicken again with the spice mixture
  8. Turn chicken pieces over and baste with the pan juices. Grill for another 10 minutes
  9. Test grilled chicken with a skewer to check if cooked

Serve with rice or bread rolls and a choice of salads and pickles.
